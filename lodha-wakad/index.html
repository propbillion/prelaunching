Questions

Answered.

What is the EOI (Token) and why does it matter?+

An Expression of Interest (EOI (Token)) registers you for priority allocation before the public launch. EOI (Token) holders select from invite-only inventory first; the remaining units open to the market at launch in November.

How much is the EOI (Token), and is it refundable?+

The EOI (Token) amount is Rs. 4.5 Lakh and is 100% refundable if you choose not to proceed. Ask our desk for the written refund terms before you submit.

When does the EOI (Token) window close?+

EOIs (Tokens) are being accepted from October to November 2026, ahead of the November launch. Bookings are attended slot-wise, so confirm your slot early.

What residences are available?+

3 BHK residences of 1,150 - 1,200 sqft carpet (indicative Rs. 1.95 Cr*) and 3.5 BHK residences of 1,450 - 1,500 sqft carpet (indicative Rs. 2.55 Cr*). Both come with two private decks and two car parks.

Where exactly is the project?+

Kasturi Chowk, Wakad, Pune - close to Hinjewadi IT Park, Phoenix Mall of the Millennium, Shivaji Chowk Metro Station and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

Is the project RERA registered?+

Yes. Wakad New Project - Tower 1 is registered with MahaRERA under PR1260002602193, promoted by Lodha Developers Limited. Verify on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

Who designed Lodha Wakad?+

Architecture is by Padma Bhushan Hafeez Contractor in an Art Deco language, and the forest-themed landscape is by Landscape Tectonix, Bangkok.
