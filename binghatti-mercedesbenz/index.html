About Mercedes-Benz Places.

Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City is a landmark residential community in Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, developed by Binghatti - a masterplanned city of 12 towers where global brand prestige becomes a personal, lived-in experience, with 12,814 homes across Studio to 5 bedroom residences.

The Grand Promenade is the emotional and social heart of the community - 12 distinct lifestyle experiences with retail, cafés and lifestyle uses that blur the boundary between indoor and outdoor space.

Is Mercedes-Benz Places currently selling? +

Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City is in its new-launch phase. Registrations are open across all 12 towers, with pricing and payment plan available on request.

Where is the project located? +

Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai - at the junction of Sheikh Zayed Road, Al Ain Road and Al Khail Road, close to the Meydan Hotel and Race Course.

What residence types are available? +

Studio to 5 bedroom residences across 12 towers - 12,814 units in total, several with private pool terraces, developed along a rational, functional grid.

What amenities does the project offer? +

A sky infinity pool, podium pool and private pools, spa service, gym, concierge and valet service, yoga & wellness outdoor area, skyline jogging path, entertainment studios, food & beverage outlets, smart home and acoustic systems, and solar photovoltaic technology.

Who is the developer? +

Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City is developed by Binghatti Holding Limited, a vertically integrated Dubai developer founded in 2008, with a total portfolio exceeding 80 projects valued at over AED 80 billion and more than 50 projects delivered to date.

What is the overall development composition? +

A masterplanned city of 12 towers anchored by the Vision Iconic star beacon tower (66 residential floors), with secure basement parking and a Grand Promenade of 12 distinct lifestyle experiences at its heart.
