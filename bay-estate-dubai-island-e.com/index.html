About Bay Estates & Nakheel.

Bay Estates is the first residential community launched on Island E at Dubai Islands, developed by Nakheel - a limited collection of 3 to 7 bedroom villas and townhouses designed around beachfront and waterfront living, with direct beach access, landscaped spaces and a waterfront promenade.

Nakheel, a member of Dubai Holding, is a leading developer with an iconic portfolio of master communities and residential, retail, hospitality and leisure developments across Dubai. Nakheel's waterfront projects, including the world-famous Palm Jumeirah, have added more than 300 kilometres to Dubai's original, 70km coastline.

Is Bay Estates currently selling? +

Bay Estates is in its new-launch phase on Island E, Dubai Islands. Registrations are open, with pricing starting from AED 4.66 million.

Where is the project located? +

Bay Estates is located on Island E, Dubai Islands - approximately 15 minutes from Dubai International Airport and Dubai Creek Harbour, and 20 minutes from DIFC and Downtown Dubai.
